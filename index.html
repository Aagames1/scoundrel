HOW TO PLAY

Descend through a dungeon built from a deck of cards: Spades (♠, 2-A), Clubs (♣, 2-A), Diamonds (♦, 2–10), and Hearts (♥, 2–10). J = 11, Q = 12, K = 13, A = 14. Your max health starts at 20.

Each room lays out four cards. Before interacting with any of them, you may flee the room once, shuffling all four cards to the bottom of the dungeon — but you can't flee two rooms in a row, and once you've resolved a card you cannot flee that room anymore.

Otherwise, resolve three of the four cards, one at a time, in whatever order you like:

  • Diamonds — Weapons. Click it to immediately equip it. Your old weapon (and any monster killed on it) is discarded.
  • Spades and Clubs — Monsters. Choose to either fight it barehanded (take its full value in damage) or with your weapon (take the monster's value minus the weapon's value, minimum zero). A weapon that has slain something can only be used on monsters of strictly lower value than the last one it killed — a Level 6 kill means you can only use it on monsters of value 5 or lower.
  • Hearts — Health Potions. Click it to heal you for its value, up to your maximum HP. You can only use one potion per room; clicking a second one causes it to be wasted.

The fourth, unplayed card carries over into the next room, joined by three new cards. You win by clearing every card from the dungeon. You lose if your health reaches zero.

Scoring: Survive and your score is your remaining health — unless you finish at the max 20 HP, and your very last card was a health potion, in which case you add that potion's value as a bonus. Fall in battle, and your score is the negative sum of every monster still left remaining in the dungeon.
